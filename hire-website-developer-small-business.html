What to Ask Before You Hire

These 5 questions will save you from expensive mistakes.

1. "What's included in the price?"

Some developers charge $299 for a "website" but that doesn't include mobile responsiveness, a contact form, or SEO setup. Ask for a specific list of what you're getting. Our $299 package includes 5 pages, mobile design, contact form, Google Maps, and free hosting setup — no surprises.

2. "How many revisions are included?"

This is the #1 hidden cost in web development. Many developers include 1-2 revisions, then charge $50-100/hour for changes after that. Unlimited revisions should be standard — if a developer won't stand behind their work, neither should you.

3. "Will I own my website?"

Some agencies build your site on proprietary platforms where you don't actually own anything. You should own your domain, your content, and have full access to your files. We deliver everything to GitHub — it's yours forever.

4. "What happens if I need changes later?"

Will you be able to make small text edits yourself? Or will you pay $100 every time you want to change a phone number? Ask about ongoing maintenance costs and whether you'll have edit access.

5. "What's the real timeline?"

Agencies often quote 2-3 months. DIY takes weekends of your time. A custom developer working solo can usually deliver in 24-48 hours for a standard 5-page site. If someone quotes you a week+ for a basic site, ask why.

Red Flags to Watch For

If you see any of these when shopping for a website developer, proceed with caution:

  • No portfolio or examples of past work
  • Vague answers about pricing ("it depends" without a range)
  • Requires full payment before starting
  • Can't explain what happens if you want changes
  • No contract or written agreement
  • Won't give you access to your own files
